Container with the most Water

in this problem we will be utilizing the concept of two pointers to determine which
will contain most of the water, we will be using the two pointers to determine the distance
of the length and the height, the length is the difference between the right pointer and the left
pointer, meanwhile the height is whatever the minimum between the height at the left pointer position
the height at the right pointer position. Once we find the area we will compare it using Python's max()
operator to find the recent area or the previous is the higher value. and we return that value.
