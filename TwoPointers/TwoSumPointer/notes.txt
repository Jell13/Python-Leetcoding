Two Sum Pointers

This problem we will utilize the concept of two pointers starting from the end and the right
and yes we are doing the same thing we done before which was two sum, before we would utilize
hash tables and then array methods but this time we will be using two pointers, the reason why 
we could do this is because for two sum the array was not in order meanwhile for this one the
list will be in order which allows us to check from high to low, and check if the result is a 
to high therefore we just go back from the right since the right will have the highest value.
The same goes to the right if the result is too low therefore we move forward from the left.
